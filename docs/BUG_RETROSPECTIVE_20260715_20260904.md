## 3. Recurring bug classes

This is the part worth reading twice. Almost every incident in the window belongs to one of fifteen classes, and most classes recurred three or more times before a structural fix landed.

### 3.1 Bare-substring keyword matching (9 incidents)

| Date | Match | Effect |
