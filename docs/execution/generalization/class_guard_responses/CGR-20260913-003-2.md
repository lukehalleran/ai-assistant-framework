## What the parent changed and why

The worker's patch filtered 'overwhelmed' through `utils.trigger_match.is_negated`. In review, the parent probed the deployed `_calculate_harm_score` on both the base module (loaded from `git show HEAD:` into a scratch file) and the candidate. The negation filter under-fires on distress:

| Message | Base | R05 worker | Corrected |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| "I can't stop feeling overwhelmed" | 2.0 | 0.0 | 2.0 |
| "never been this overwhelmed" | 2.0 | 0.0 | 2.0 |
| "without help I'm overwhelmed" | 2.0 | 0.0 | 2.0 |
| "I don't feel overwhelmed" | 2.0 | 0.0 | 2.0 |
| "not overwhelmed" | 2.0 | 2.0 | 2.0 |
| "unhappy and overwhelmed" | 0.0 | 2.0 | 2.0 |
| "overwhelmed by all these gifts" | 0.0 | 0.0 | 0.0 |

Why the filter is removed:

- **Siblings ignore negation.** `_calculate_harm_score` scans its HIGH/MEDIUM/CONCERN/EVENT vocabularies without negation: `iter_hits` with no negation filter. A negation filter on 'overwhelmed' alone is a one-off rule.
- **The cue list is the wrong tool.** `NEGATION_CUE_RE` holds request-scoping cues (`stop`, `never`, `without`, `skip`, `avoid`, ...). On affective statements those words intensify the distress ("can't stop feeling") or scope a different word ("without help"). Filtering on them drops real distress, and under-firing is the BC-28 direction that matters.
- **Parent's error, not the worker's.** The parent's own R05 contract asked for negation-awareness. This corrects that contract; the worker did not deviate from it.
