## Roster decision (contract point 3, brief-mandated record)

A roster-only failure (the documents leg itself healthy or no_results, but
the metadata-only roster fetch raises) marks the **whole** `user_uploads`
section as NOT CHECKED — i.e. `failed` with reason `"roster:<ExcClass>"` —
even though the documents leg's own items are real and are kept in the
returned list. This is the F3b precedent (a partial-leg failure still marks
the section failed while keeping the other leg's items, matching
`get_notes`' keyword/semantic split). The alternative (recording the roster
failure as a separate, lower-severity signal that does not flip the whole
section's status) was not implemented, since `_section_outcomes` (F5) has no
sub-section granularity to carry that distinction, and the roster row and
the retrieved uploads render in the same `[USER UPLOADED ITEMS]` block (F6a
consumer). **The class-guard/parent reviews this choice** per the brief.
